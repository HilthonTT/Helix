# Settings & themes

Settings is in the sidebar, and **File → Preferences** opens it from the dashboard. It has two halves: your **account** and your **preferences**. Preferences are per account, except the theme.

<Media src="settings.png" alt="The preferences page with switches and numeric fields" caption="Preferences: switches above, numbers below." />

## Switches

| Setting | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Auto connect drives on launch** | connect your drives as soon as you sign in. Drives with **Connect automatically** off, and pinned drives away from home, are left out |
| **Auto minimize app** | hide Helix to the tray after a countdown on the dashboard |
| **Close to the system tray** | on by default: the close button hides to the tray instead of quitting. Windows only |
| **Notify when hidden to the tray** | on by default: say so the first time Helix hides itself each session. Windows only |
| **Global shortcuts** | off by default: <Kbd>Ctrl</Kbd>+<Kbd>Alt</Kbd>+<Kbd>Shift</Kbd>+<Kbd>C</Kbd> / <Kbd>D</Kbd> from any app — see [Tray & shortcuts](/docs/tray-and-shortcuts/) |
| **Set on startup** | start Helix when you sign in to your computer (a Startup-folder shortcut on Windows, a LaunchAgent on macOS) |
| **Set desktop shortcut** | keep a Helix shortcut on your desktop |

A switch takes effect the moment you flip it.

## Numbers

Each number has a stepper and a unit; typing a value saves it when you press <Kbd>Enter</Kbd> or leave the field. Several have a meaning for zero.

| Setting | Default | Zero means |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Timer count in seconds** — the auto-minimize countdown | 15 seconds | — |
| **Audit log retention** — days of history to keep | 90 days | keep everything |
| **Low space warning** — warn when a volume has less than this much free | 10% | off |
| **Lock when idle** — ask for the password after this many minutes without input | 0 | never lock |

### The auto-minimize countdown

With **Auto minimize app** on, the dashboard header shows a small countdown chip (`m:ss`). When it reaches zero the window hides to the tray. Cancel it from the chip; it only runs while the dashboard is on screen.

### The low-space warning

Every 15 minutes Helix measures the free space on the volumes your connected drives sit on. When one falls below the threshold you get a notification naming the drives on it, and the sidebar's warning light comes on. A volume is reported once, not every quarter of an hour, and can warn again after it has recovered.

### The idle lock

After the set number of minutes without keyboard or mouse input **anywhere on the computer**, Helix covers itself with a lock screen and asks for your password. It is a lock, not a sign-out: your drives stay mounted, and reconnects and schedules carry on behind it. The **Lock** button in the sidebar locks immediately, whatever this setting says. A forgotten password? Sign out from the lock screen and use **Forgot your password?**.

## Themes

| Theme | Look |
| --- | --- |
| **Crimson** | the default: near-black with a crimson accent |
| **System** | Light or Dark, following your operating system |
| **Light** | light |
| **Dark** | dark |
| **Midnight**, **Amethyst**, **Sand**, **Glacier** | custom palettes |

The theme applies at once, including to the sign-in and lock pages, and is remembered per user of the computer rather than per Helix account.

## Language

English, French, German, Dutch, Indonesian and Japanese. The audit log is shown in the language you pick, including entries written before you changed it.

## Diagnostic logs

**Export** saves Helix's log files — 14 days of what it did, including unattended reconnects — as a zip in a folder you choose, to attach to a bug report. See [Troubleshooting](/docs/troubleshooting/).

## Updates

**Check for updates** compares your version with the latest release on GitHub and, when a newer one exists, offers to download and install it. See [Installation](/docs/installation/).

## Account

| Item | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Username** | change your username |
| **Password** | change your password; asks for the current one |
| **Recovery key** | **Create a new recovery key** after entering your password. The old key stops working. Accounts created before recovery keys existed should make one — Helix reminds you at every sign-in until you do |
