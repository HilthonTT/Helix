# Insights

**Insights**, in the sidebar, shows two things Helix records in the background: how full each NAS volume has been over time, and which drives have been dropping their connection.

## Storage over time

Helix measures each connected volume up to four times a day while it runs and keeps one reading per volume per day, for about thirteen months. Insights charts those readings, one chart per volume.

| Control | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **30 days** / **90 days** / **1 year** | how much history the charts show. 90 days is the default |

Each volume shows:

- **how full it is now**, as a percentage and as used against total space,
- **a trend line**: *Free space falling by 3.2 GB a day*, *Free space is steady or growing*, or *Not enough history for a trend yet*,
- **the chart**, plotting the percentage used, so a 2 TB volume and a 40 TB pool share one scale,
- **the span** of the readings shown, and how many there are.

Volumes that will fill up soonest come first.

The trend always comes from the **last 30 days**, whichever range you are looking at. It is the same figure behind the *Full in about … at the current rate* line on the [dashboard](/docs/dashboard/), so the two never disagree. A year of history is there to look at, not to predict from.

A volume is recognized by its total size, the same way the dashboard counts volumes. If you grow a volume it starts a new history, and the old one ages out.

If the page says **Nothing recorded yet**, Helix has not yet measured a connected drive. Leave it running with your drives connected and the charts fill in from the first day.

## Drive reliability

The second half lists every drive that **lost its connection** in the last 30 days, with:

- how many times it dropped, and how many of those were in the last week,
- when it last dropped,
- whether it came back on its own, and how quickly it usually did.

A drive that dropped three or more times in the last week is marked with a warning. Drives that never dropped are left out, so the one that keeps disconnecting is not buried among thirteen that never did. If none dropped, the page says so.

The figures come from the [audit log](/docs/settings/) and use the same counting as the reliability lines in [Diagnosing a drive](/docs/diagnosing/). Drops are only noticed while Helix is running: a drive that went down while the app was closed is not counted.
